The World That Sustains Us

Before monuments, our planet Earth composed its own masterpieces. The Great Barrier Reef, Serengeti National Park, Socotra Archipelago, Wulingyuan Scenic Area, Lake Turkana National Parks, and Grand Canyon National Park reveal ecosystems shaped through deep time. Coral reefs, savannas, forests, and canyons sustain life while reminding us that humanity exists within nature’s vast living design.
