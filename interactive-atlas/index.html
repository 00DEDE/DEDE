As UNESCO celebrates eighty years of safeguarding what makes us human, we are reminded how it takes each and every one of us to weave the fabric of human existence.

What is in front of you was originally born from ink and paper, but this atlas now breathes. It has become a living map where heritage pulses across borders, where monuments and languages whisper to each other, and where nature holds hands with tradition. Every site, every song, every syllable carries the fingerprint of someone who believed it was worth preserving.

This digital atlas is not a record of the past. It is a promise to the future and the next generations to come. This is our shared world, a world always connected, always changing, always growing.

As we conclude our journey around the world, all of us here at UNESCO would like to remind you that this is all possible because of you. People around the world just like you and I have taken time to preserve, protect and prepare our shared inheritance for the benefit of future generations.

The built monuments, the natural sites, the intangible traditions and all of our diverse languages are what makes us human. The future is imminent and it is our responsibiliy to bring these four worlds with us. Always remember, the world needs U.
