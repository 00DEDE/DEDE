LIVING TRACES gathers languages and traditions that still breathe, even as many stand at the edge of disappearance. Across oceans, deserts, forests, mountains, and ice, communities continue to sing, weave, heal, dive, carve, remember, and speak words that cannot be replaced. This pamphlet shines light on a few of the many living inheritances that will form part of the exhibition. What you will see here are eight intangible heritages: four endangered languages and four fragile cultural practices, each carrying memory, knowledge, and identity across generations.The exhibition will be active at The Met from March 16 to June 3, 2027. We invite you to encounter heritage not as something that is finished or distant, but as something human, vulnerable, and still being kept alive by many and by few.
