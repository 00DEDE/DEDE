TRADITIONS

Location: UgandaBigwala is a royal music tradition performed with gourd trum-pets during ceremonies of the Busoga Kingdom. Once central to cultural identity, it now faces decline due to reduced patronage and aging practitioners. Safeguarding efforts focus on transmis-sion, ensuring that its powerful sound continues to resonate across generations.

Location: MaliSanké Mon is a communal fishing ritual marking seasonal cycles and social unity. Thousands gather in coordinated action, blending ecology, spirituality, and celebration. Environmental pressures and social change threaten its continuity, making safeguarding essential to preserving both the practice and its connection to identity.
