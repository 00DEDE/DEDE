Tani in Malagasy

Means Earth In English

Kabary, the traditional Malagasy oratory art, was inscribed on the UNESCO Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2021. It is a highly structured, poetic, and conversational form of speech used in ceremonies like weddings and funerals to maintain social cohesion, using proverbs, metaphors, and allegories to convey wisdom, respect, and community values.
