Monde in French

Means World In English

Grasse, the world’s perfume capital, combines centuries-old, UNESCO-recognized expertise in cultivating fragrant plants (like jasmine and rose) with traditional processing and creative composition. Grasse boasts numerous historic perfume houses (Fragonard, Molinard, Galimard) that continue to hand-harvest ingredients and produce high-end scents, offering immersive workshops for making custom perfumes.
