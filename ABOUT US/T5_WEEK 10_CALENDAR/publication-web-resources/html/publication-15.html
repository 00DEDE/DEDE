Hati in Indonesian

Means Heart In English

Indonesian Batik is a UNESCO-recognized Intangible Cultural Heritage (since 2009) textile art, characterized by intricate designs created using hot wax resist-dyeing, often using a canting (pen) or cap (stamp). It is deeply rooted in Javanese culture, symbolizing local wisdom, philosophy, and history, with popular motifs including Kawung, Parang, and Mega Mendung.
