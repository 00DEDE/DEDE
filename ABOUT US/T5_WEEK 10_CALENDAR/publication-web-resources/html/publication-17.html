The Carnival of Barranquilla is Colombia’s biggest cultural party, declared a UNESCO Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity in 2003. Held annually before Lent, this four-day, second-largest carnival in the world merges Indigenous, African, and European traditions, featuring vibrant parades like the Battle of the Flowers, cumbia music, and elaborate costumes in Barranquilla.
