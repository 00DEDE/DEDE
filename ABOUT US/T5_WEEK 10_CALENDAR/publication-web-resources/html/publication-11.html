Qorax in Somali

Means Sun In English

Xeer Ciise, the oral customary laws of the Somali-Issa communities, was inscribed on the UNESCO Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2024, it was jointly nominated by Ethiopia, Djibouti, and Somalia. This structured system governs social, economic, and political relations across borders, focusing on conflict resolution, peace, and traditional governance.
